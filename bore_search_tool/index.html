This tool shows whether OGIA's predicts that coal seam gas water extraction will lower the water level in a bore, by how much and roughly when. It covers registered bores in the Surat Cumulative Management Area only.

Where do I find my RN?

The registration number (RN) is a 2 to 10 digit number printed on your bore card, water licence, driller's report or correspondence from OGIA or the gas company. If you cannot find it, use the address search above to list bores near your property.
