    Surat Underground

    A 3D explorer for the Office of Groundwater Impact Assessment (OGIA) 2017 regional geological model of the Surat Cumulative Management Area (Rev2B, exported from Petrel). 1836 × 2598 × 21 cells at 250 m, decimated to 1 km and 2 km for the web.
